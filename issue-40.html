1.28 Billion Euros for the Eyes of the Missile

Marcus Vail Resilience Alpha Research November 17, 2026 4 min read

ASELSAN told Borsa Istanbul on Monday, November 16, 2026 that it had signed contracts with ROKETSAN worth 1,281,101,744 euros for missile data link and seeker systems. The disclosure, filed on the Public Disclosure Platform, known as KAP, gives the value to the last euro, names ROKETSAN as the customer, and sets November 16 as the start of the relationship. It says the work will have a positive effect on revenue. The filing is summarised here, with the KAP disclosure as the primary source.

The consensus read

The quick read in Ankara and in the defence press is simple. Turkey is buying guidance at scale at home, so Turkish missiles no longer depend on a foreign supplier for the parts that find the target and talk to the launcher. A seeker sits in the nose of a missile and locks on in the final phase of flight. A data link is the two-way radio between the missile in flight and the aircraft or launcher that fired it. Put together, they decide whether a missile that leaves the rail has a current target picture when it arrives.

That read has evidence behind it. In July 2025, at IDEF in Istanbul, the Presidency of Defence Industries, ASELSAN and ROKETSAN signed a development contract for an active electronically scanned array seeker, AGRAS 3101/AA, and a missile data link for the GOKBORA long range air to air missile. ASELSAN called that seeker the first of its kind developed nationally in Turkey. The signatories named in that release were SSB President Haluk Gorgun, ASELSAN Chief Executive Ahmet Akyol and ROKETSAN General Manager Murat Ikinci. Monday is a much larger number attached to the same two product lines.

What the filing does not say

Here is where I part company with the headline trade. The November 16 filing names no missile. It gives no quantity, no delivery schedule, no split between seekers and data links, and no end user. It does not say the contracts cover GOKBORA, and ASELSAN has not said so. Without a delivery schedule you cannot place the 1.28 billion euros in 2027, 2029 or 2032 revenue with any honesty. Anyone who models it as near term backlog on a straight line is guessing.

Compare the paper trail. In June 2024 ASELSAN disclosed ROKETSAN contracts and amendments for missile subsystems and seeker production worth 20,149,637.46 dollars, 45,697,336.60 euros and 476,316,251 Turkish lira, with deliveries stated as 2024 to 2028. In 2016 it disclosed an infrared seeker contract for the KARAOK anti tank system worth 7,451,002 euros plus 43,994,661 lira, with deliveries planned for 2018 to 2022. Those filings gave dates. Monday gave a total and a start date, and stopped.

The one number that does move

ROKETSAN accounted for 5.6 percent of ASELSAN net sales in its latest published income statement and 7.3 percent of trade receivables on its latest balance sheet, according to the same disclosure. In June 2024 that sales figure was 2 percent. The customer is not new. Its weight inside ASELSAN is rising, and Monday adds a contract equal to that relationship many times over, in euros, which matters for a Turkish defence exporter that reports in lira and sells guidance electronics priced in hard currency.

My position: own the bottleneck, not the timetable. Seekers and data links are the components a missile maker can least easily substitute when an export licence is refused, and ASELSAN is now contracted to supply them to Turkey's principal missile maker at a scale no earlier KAP filing comes close to. That is a real position in the supply chain. It still leaves no dated revenue stream you can defend in a model.

Watch for the follow on paper, not the next headline. The markers are a KAP filing that names a programme or a delivery window for these contracts, ASELSAN quarterly results that show ROKETSAN rising above 5.6 percent of sales, and any SSB statement that ties this order to a named missile in production. Until one of those lands, the honest answer on timing is nobody knows yet.

ASELS · ASELSAN Elektronik Sanayi ve Ticaret A.S.
