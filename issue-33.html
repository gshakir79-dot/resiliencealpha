Terbium Has a $2,050 Floor Now, and Washington Wrote It

Marcus Vail Resilience Alpha Research November 10, 2026 3 min read

USA Rare Earth (Nasdaq: USAR) announced on Monday, November 9 that its Serra Verde operation in Brazil has begun commercial scale shipments of mixed rare earth carbonate, and started receiving payments in November. Shipments began in October, after Stage 1 of the Serra Verde optimization program was completed, which the company says was within budget and on time, and the product meets the buyer quality specifications in the contract. The full release is here, via Globe Newswire.

What happened

The buyer is US SIIE, LLC, a special purpose vehicle funded by the US Government. The agreement runs 15 years and takes 100 percent of Phase I production on a take or pay basis. Serra Verde, in Goias state, mines heavy rare earth rich ionic clays at Pela Ema and is, in the company words, the first scaled processor of that feedstock outside Asia. The carbonate contains all four magnet elements: neodymium, praseodymium, dysprosium and terbium, plus yttrium and other heavy rare earths.

The one number set that matters

The floors. The contract guarantees $110 per kilogram for neodymium and for praseodymium, $575 per kilogram for dysprosium, and $2,050 per kilogram for terbium. Above the floors, USAR is paid 70 percent of the non China market price in excess of the floor, less a fixed separation cost, with market price set from defined European and US reference prices. The release puts recent non China dysprosium oxide at 14 to 16 times the China price, and terbium oxide at 6 to 9 times, citing Argus and Benchmark Mineral Intelligence indices from late October. Price insurance of this kind is the point. A mine that must sell dysprosium against a Chinese reference price has no bankable cash flow. A mine with a 15 year floor and a government funded buyer does, provided it ships.

What is not proven yet

Volume. The release discloses no shipped tonnage and no revenue figure for the first sales. The 4,000 tonnes per year total rare earth oxide run rate is a target for the second quarter of 2027, not a current rate. Stage 2, planned for commissioning in the third quarter of 2027, targets about 6,400 tonnes per year by the end of 2027, and a possible Phase II expansion that could double run of mine production is still at technoeconomic evaluation. The release own risk section lists the hard caveats in plain language: the offtake can terminate, the counterparty must stay capitalized, and definitive debt financing tied to the arrangement still has to be finalized on the contemplated timetable. Ricardo Grossi, the company head of upstream in Brazil, offered the human detail that tends to survive ramp ups: about 70 percent of the skilled workforce at Serra Verde comes from the Minacu community nearby, and product quality, he said, is running ahead of planned specification. Local crews usually decide whether a ramp holds.

My position, and what to watch

My position: the shipment is proof of production, and the floors are the investable fact. Washington has stopped asking the market to build a non China heavy rare earth supply chain on hope and has instead written the price support into a 15 year contract. That changes how I read every other Western rare earth developer still selling a resource estimate and a rendering. Watch three markers. First, USAR next quarterly filing, for the first disclosed revenue and tonnage from these shipments. Second, any notice on the debt financing the risk section flags. Third, the run rate prints on the way to the second quarter of 2027 target. If the tonnage follows the paperwork, this contract becomes the template. If it does not, the floors were a subsidy in search of a mine.

The company covered in this issue:

USAR · USA Rare Earth, Inc.

Nasdaq: USAR · Owner of Serra Verde and the Pela Ema mine in Brazil, Less Common Metals in the United Kingdom, and magnet capacity in Stillwater, Oklahoma · 15 year, 100 percent take or pay offtake to a US Government funded buyer, with floors at $110/kg Nd and Pr, $575/kg Dy and $2,050/kg Tb
